# Design - VCA Veilig & Vakkundig B.V.

A locked design system for the full website. The site should feel like a Dutch public institution for safety certification: official, calm, structured, and expensive without becoming blank.

## Genre
modern-minimal, government registry, document-led.

## Macrostructure Family
- Marketing pages: Government Registry with asymmetrical document panels, proof bands, and official folder cards.
- App pages: Workbench/registry lists with status indicators and strict horizontal rules.
- Content pages: Long document with clear section hierarchy and restrained proof blocks.

## Theme
- `--color-paper`: `#f2f4f7`
- `--color-paper-2`: `#ffffff`
- `--color-ink`: `#1a2634`
- `--color-ink-2`: `#4a5568`
- `--color-rule`: `#d1d5db`
- `--color-navy`: `#0b2b44`
- `--color-accent`: `#d45213`
- `--color-focus`: `#d45213`

## Typography
- Display: Inter, weight 800, normal.
- Body: Inter, weight 400-600.
- Mono: system mono only for registration-like labels.
- H1 desktop target: 5xl to 6xl.
- No italic headings. No pale gray descriptions.

## Spacing
4/8-point scale via `tokens.css`. Sections use strong rhythm: compact official headers, wider registry blocks, crisp dividers.

## Motion
Conservative. Subtle transform/opacity only. The 3D hero is delayed and decorative.

## Microinteractions
Buttons feel official: orange filled CTA, navy outline secondary, immediate focus ring, no bounce.

## What Pages Must Share
Soft cool paper background, navy top-bar cards, orange CTA usage, dark readable copy, sharp official layout, and document-like dividers.
